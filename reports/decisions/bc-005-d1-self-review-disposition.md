establish that every self-review method fails, that a different model would fail,
or that context organization and policy wording have no effect. R3 describes a
required post-shift state; deriving the pre-shift healthy-capacity checkpoint was
an explicitly approved part of the prospective evaluation design. Discussion of
more explicit action-precondition wording does not retrospectively change that design.

Fixed-seed repeats are not independent samples. Confidence HIGH is an uncalibrated
self-report. Runtime containment prevented no attempted unsafe operational action:
there was only proposal evaluation, no tool execution. ALLOW did not validate the
free-text action sequence. No general operational safety claim follows.
